A self-taught practice across short documentary, music video, and photographic series — built outside any film school program, driven by curiosity about how cinema's grammar translates into work that lives on social platforms without losing narrative density.

## The documentary

Embedded above: a documentary short shot and edited end-to-end. The piece grew out of a longer interview process — the cut is structured around moments where the subject contradicts their own framing, because those are the moments that read as true. Sound design done in-camera; color in DaVinci Resolve.

> The job of editing isn't to make the strongest take feel strongest. It's to find the take where the camera caught the person before they remembered they were being filmed.

## Music video work

Ongoing collaborations with independent musicians. Treatments are written first, shotlists second — the constraint is always: what's the one image this song needs that you can't get from a stock library? Built in Capcut and Premiere depending on turnaround.

## Photographic series

The series approach — six to twelve images, one subject, one week — is where the eye gets trained for everything else. The Caracas essay (see [Caracas case study](/javiport/en/work/caracas-photo-essay/)) came out of this practice.
