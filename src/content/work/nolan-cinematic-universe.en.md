Senior thesis written at Colegio Los Arcos analyzing how Christopher Nolan's filmography constructs a coherent narrative architecture across non-franchise films — and how that architecture builds the kind of fan loyalty usually reserved for connected franchises.

The piece argues that Nolan's recurring devices — non-linear time, ensembles of expertise, moral arithmetic under duress — function as a brand grammar. The same grammar, applied at smaller scale, is what gives independent creators their own gravitational pull.
