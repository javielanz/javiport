The Streets of Caracas

A documentary series on the city as it is, not as it's reported.

ROLE Photographer, Editor
YEAR 2025
LOCATION Caracas, Venezuela
FRAMES ~40 selected

Most photography of Caracas is reactive — protests, queues, scarcity. This series isn't. It's the city on a Tuesday morning. It explores the quiet resilience, the textures of daily life, and the enduring spirit of a place often misunderstood by the global narrative.

"The city doesn't pose. You wait."
— J.L.

Shot exclusively on the Fujifilm X-T4 with a 23mm lens, emphasizing constraints to force tighter composition. Processed in Lightroom to bring out the natural tones of the early morning light without artificially boosting contrast.
