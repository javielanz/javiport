Street-journalism projects at Universidad Monteávila: going out with a notebook and a recorder to interview strangers in Caracas. No appointment, no fixed script, no guarantee anyone wants to talk to you.

## The work

- **Cold approach** — stopping someone who isn't expecting you, introducing yourself, and earning 2 minutes of their attention with the first sentence.
- **Active listening** — the follow-up question is worth more than the question list. The good material shows up when the interviewee notices you're actually listening.
- **Field reporting** — verifying names, dates, and context on site, with the noise of the street working against you.

## Team pieces

The pieces are published as a team: individual reporting, collective editing. I collaborated on publications that address local issues through a narrative angle — one person's story as the way into a city-wide subject.

## Why it's in this portfolio

This is where my ease in face-to-face commercial work comes from. Prospecting and cold approaches are the same muscle: walking up to someone who wasn't expecting you, reading the reaction, adjusting your tone, and keeping the conversation alive. I trained it on the street before using it in pitches.
