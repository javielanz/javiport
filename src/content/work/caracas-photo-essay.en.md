Most photography of Caracas is reactive — protests, queues, scarcity. This series isn't. It's the city on a Tuesday morning: motorcycles parked outside half-painted shutters, neighbors crossing intersections that don't need lights, the texture of a place that lives on regardless of what's being reported about it.

The subject is a single figure carrying a toolbox through her neighborhood. The frame is wide, the color is candy, the pace is slow. Shot over six weekends with a Fujifilm X-T4 and a 23mm lens. Edited in Capcut and Lightroom.
